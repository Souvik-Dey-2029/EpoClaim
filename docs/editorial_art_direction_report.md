## 1. Executive Summary & Design Reset

The landing page has undergone a complete art-direction reset, moving completely away from the generic AI/SaaS template (left headline, floating marketing stat cards, excessive pill buttons, artificial percentages, and repetitive card carousels).

In its place, EpoClaim now presents as a **Technical Editorial Research Instrument**, inspired by the visual gravity of the design references:
- **Atmospheric Dark Forest Ground:** Deep `#103D2D` to `#0B1B15` canvas with volumetric light and organic depth.
- **Warm Tactile Specimen Interrupting the Canvas:** A physical warm-beige (`#F1E7D1`) computational specimen surface (FIG 01) directly embedded on the landing page, demonstrating genuine decomposition mechanics.
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Masthead:** High-contrast display typography (`Space Grotesk` with an italic accent) paired with a contextual research folio annotation and structured action controls.
- **Three Core Technical Instruments:** Deep technical explainers replacing generic marketing cards, detailing Four-Clock Reference Alignment, Coordinate & Possessive Splitting, and Evidence Clustering.
- **Zero Fabricated Statistics:** Removed the artificial "100% PROPOSITION SPAN GROUNDING" lead statistic. Replaced with empirical data, exact temporal intervals `[t_s, t_e]`, and verified regression truth tables.

## 3. Real Technical Visualizations Implemented

The center of the new landing page is **FIG 01: Atomic Decomposition & Temporal Discord Specimen**, which models the verified backend architecture from `backend/app/services/claim_extraction/proposition_decomposer.py` and `backend/app/services/temporal_analysis/service.py`:

1. **Specimen Statement:**
   > *"OpenAI is based in San Francisco, and its interim CEO is Mira Murati."*
2. **Clause Splitting:**
   - **Clause A (Green highlight):** `OpenAI is based in San Francisco` → Isolated as `P1 • ATOMIC FACT`, classified as `SLOW_MOVING / TIME_INVARIANT`, valid span `[2015-12-11, Present]`, verdict `SUPPORTED`.
   - **Clause B (Red highlight):** `its interim CEO is Mira Murati` → Isolated as `P2 • ATOMIC FACT`, classified as `FAST_MOVING (Executive Title)`, valid window `[2023-11-17 → 2023-11-20] (EXPIRED)`, verdict `OUTDATED / SUPERSEDED`.
3. **Interactive Filter:** Users can click `Both Clauses`, `Clause A (Invariant)`, or `Clause B (Superseded)` to dynamically dim non-matching clauses and proposition cards in real-time.
4. **Automated Parent Synthesis:**
   - Evaluates the truth table: `P(A)=SUPPORTED ∧ P(B)=OUTDATED ⇒ PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`.
   - Displays the exact grounded factual reconstruction generated by the system.
